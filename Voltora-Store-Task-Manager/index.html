About Voltora

Voltora is a fictional electronics store. The Staff & Task Manager helps the manager and the team plan the day: who is working, what needs doing, what is overdue, and what the store is spending on running costs.

It was built as a front-end prototype for IMS566 Advanced Web Design Development and Content Management.

What you can do

60 repair services

A customer catalogue for phones, laptops, PCs, accessories, software and cameras with request and tracking.

Task management

Create, assign, search and filter tasks, then update progress and status.

Staff & performance

Profiles with workload, completion rate, current tasks and weekly shifts.

Schedule

Calendar and timeline of deadlines, important dates and the weekly roster.

Store expenses

Record utilities, maintenance and supplies against a monthly budget.

Live dashboard

Interactive charts that update with period, staff and status filters.

Reports

Task, staff and finance reports with CSV export and print.

Two roles

Administrator

Manages staff, creates and assigns tasks, edits the roster, records expenses and reads every report.

Staff

Sees only their own tasks, updates progress and status, checks the schedule and reviews their own performance.
